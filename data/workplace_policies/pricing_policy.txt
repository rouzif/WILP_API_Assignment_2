Ad Tech Solutions - Pricing Policy:
1. Pricing operates on Cost-Per-Click (CPC), Cost-Per-Mille (CPM - per 1,000 impressions), or Cost-Per-Acquisition (CPA).
2. Advertisers set maximum bid limits and daily spending caps. You are charged only when an ad interaction or impression occurs according to your chosen bidding strategy.
3. Minimum campaign entry threshold is $10/day for search ads and $25/day for video campaign placements.
