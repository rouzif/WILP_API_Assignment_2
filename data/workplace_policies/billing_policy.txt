Ad Tech Solutions - Billing Policy:
1. Automatic billing occurs when your spend reaches your account's Billing Threshold (e.g., $500) or on the 1st of every calendar month, whichever comes first.
2. Accepted payment methods include corporate credit cards, wire transfers, and direct debit (ACH).
3. If a payment fails, ad serving is paused immediately. A 3-day grace period is granted before account suspension.
4. Refunds for unused pre-paid account balances take 10 to 14 business days after account closure approval.
