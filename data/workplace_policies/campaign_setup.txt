Ad Tech Solutions - Campaign Setup Policy:
1. To launch a new ad campaign, log into your Business Manager dashboard and navigate to Campaigns > Create New.
2. Select your campaign objective: Brand Awareness, Lead Generation, or Traffic/Conversions.
3. Configure daily budget limits, start/end dates, target geographical locations, and audience demographics.
4. Ad creative assets (banners, copy, video URLs) must be uploaded at least 24 hours prior to launch for automated and manual policy review.
