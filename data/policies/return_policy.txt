ShopEase Return and Refund Policy:
1. Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
2. Returned items must be unused, in original packaging, and include original tags.
3. Final sale and custom electronic items are strictly non-refundable.
4. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after receipt.
5. Standard return shipping fee is $4.99 unless the item arrived damaged or defective.
