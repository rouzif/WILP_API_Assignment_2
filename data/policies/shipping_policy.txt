ShopEase Shipping and Delivery Policy:
1. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days across the continental US and costs $5.99 (Free on orders over $50).
2. Express 2-day shipping is available at checkout for $14.99.
3. Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST ship out the same business day.
4. International shipping takes 7 to 14 business days and varies by destination customs fees.
5. Tracking numbers are emailed within 24 hours of dispatch.
